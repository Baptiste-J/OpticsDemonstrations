Diffraction by two slits

Geometry: the two slits and the observation screen

Light travels along +z, passes through the two slits and forms the pattern on the screen. The slits are long along y, so the pattern varies only along x.

Interference and diffraction pattern on the screen

Intensity along the x axis

The square is 40 mm × 40 mm. The slit-to-screen distance is fixed at L = 1 m (far-field regime). Intensity is normalised to the central maximum. The dashed curve is the single-slit diffraction envelope.

Lowering it saturates the central peak so the secondary lobes become visible (screen view and plot).
