Diffraction grating

Geometry: the grating in the yz plane

View in the yz plane; the slits are long along x (out of the page). A plane wave arrives along +z. Click or drag in the figure to change the angle θ, measured from the z axis (positive upwards). The strip on the screen shows the pattern; each wavelength is drawn in its own colour, and the colours add up.

Pattern as a function of the angle θ

Top: the pattern as it appears on a screen. Below: the intensity, normalised to the principal maximum. Each wavelength has its own curve (with up to three wavelengths, its dashed envelope and order lines too). Because different wavelengths do not interfere, the intensities simply add on the screen. Click or drag to set the angle θ (orange).

Lowering it makes weak maxima visible.
