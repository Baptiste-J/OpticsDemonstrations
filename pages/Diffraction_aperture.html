Diffraction by an aperture of arbitrary shape

Aperture: draw a shape or choose a preset

White = open, black = opaque. The grid is 128 × 128 cells. Click a black cell to open it or a white cell to block it, and drag to paint with the same action.

Diffraction pattern on the screen

Far-field (Fraunhofer) intensity, computed as the squared modulus of the 2D Fourier transform of the aperture. The screen is 40 mm × 40 mm at L = 1 m, and the intensity is normalised to the central maximum. Click or drag on the pattern to place the dashed lines: the plot below shows the intensity along the horizontal line, and the plot on the right along the vertical line (positions in mm).

Lowering it saturates the central peak so that weak features become visible.
