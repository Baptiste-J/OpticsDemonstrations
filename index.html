Interactive optics demonstrations

Short fundamental optics simulations to explore polarisation, interference and diffraction. Everything runs in your browser: there is nothing to install and no login. Best viewed on a computer, but they also work on tablets and phones.

Polarisation

Superposition of linear polarisations

Add two orthogonal linear fields and see the resulting polarisation appear.

Vary: relative amplitude, relative phase

Superposition of circular polarisations

Combine right and left circular polarisations and see the resulting polarisation appear.

Vary: relative amplitude, relative phase

Interference

Interference of two plane waves

Choose the direction of two waves in 3D and see the fringes they form on a screen.

Vary: both wave directions, relative amplitude, wavelength

Diffraction

Diffraction by a single slit

Far-field diffraction pattern of one slit in the Fraunhofer approximation.

Vary: slit width, wavelength, display range

Diffraction by two slits

Far-field diffraction pattern of two slits (Young's slits) in the Fraunhofer approximation.

Vary: slit width, slit separation, wavelength

Diffraction by an aperture.

Draw your own aperture on a grid and see the resulting far field diffraction pattern in the Fraunhofer approximation.

Vary: the shape, its size, wavelength

Diffraction grating

See the far field diffraction pattern by a grating made of N slits.

Vary: number of slits, spacing, slit width, wavelengths
